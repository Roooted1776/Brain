How Brain works (step by step)
  1. Watch. Brain reviews recent activity in the background: your sessions, connected tools, files you create, and your corrections. Incognito sessions are ignored.
  2. Write. It writes what it learns as linked entries, each pointing to its source: Concepts, Entities and Workstreams.
  3. Maintain. Every run reinforces what is still true, updates what changed, and marks stale what is not.
  4. You stay in charge. Open, edit or delete any entry. Corrections feed the next run, and a deleted entry is not learned again.
  5. Memory only. Brain does not take actions or message you.

    Instructions

    The article shows an Instructions field only in a screenshot and does not explain it. Here the text is saved and shown in each run. It does not change what is learned.

    Privacy

    • Brain learns only from your own activity, in this browser.
    • Incognito sessions are skipped completely.
    • Common password and key patterns are filtered out before saving. This is a best-effort filter, not a guarantee, so do not paste real secrets.
    • Brain only maintains memory. It does not take actions or message you.
