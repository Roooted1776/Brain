Waiting for the first task. Tasks start on their own. Each program speaks in turn, a packet of light travels along the line when work is handed over, and when a task ends the Brain learns from it in the background.
How Brain works (step by step)
  1. Watch. Brain reviews recent activity in the background: your sessions, connected tools, files you create, and your corrections. Incognito sessions are ignored.
  2. Write. It writes what it learns as linked entries, each pointing to its source: Concepts, Entities and Workstreams.
  3. Maintain. Every run reinforces what is still true, updates what changed, and marks stale what is not.
  4. You stay in charge. Open, edit or delete any entry. Your corrections feed forward into the next run.
  5. Memory only. Brain does not take actions or message you.

This page's own ideas (not in the article): the animations, the black-and-white look, the drifting network and packets of light, the graph shapes and layout; there is no question box and tasks run on their own from an automatic queue; the Plan / Queries / Sources / Answer task with cited answers; the “Report” step of a run; the Skills filter; the incognito switch and “Run Brain now” button; sample connectors; the 30-day stale rule and the “skip ahead” demo clock (the article gives no time rule); the rule that a deleted entry is not learned again; and the optional Live AI mode that lets real models from four companies voice the bots; and the password filter, which is simple pattern matching, not the AI-based filtering the article describes.

    Brain settings

    Instructions

    The article shows an Instructions field only in a screenshot and does not explain it. Here the text is saved and shown in each run. It does not change what is learned.

    Privacy

    • Brain learns only from your own activity, in this browser.
    • Incognito sessions are skipped completely.
    • Common password and key patterns are filtered out before saving. This is a best-effort filter, not a guarantee, so do not paste real secrets.
    • Brain only maintains memory. It does not take actions or message you.
    • This page is a simulation unless you switch on Live AI below. Even then, web search is never used, and nothing connects to Perplexity.
