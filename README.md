# Brain

Max's **Orchestration Brain**. Open `index.html` in any browser (double-click it). No install, no keys, free.

## What it does

- **Draggable Brain** on top of Max's team chart: Grok (lead) → Claude (hub) → Cursor, Gemini, ChatGPT (collaborators), with Muse auditing everyone.
- **Ask a question** and watch the four phases play out as a conversation between the programs: plan, 3–5 searches, source check, cited answer. Arrows on the chart light up for each hand-off.
- **Skills memory**: add, edit, delete and filter the skills you want to remember. Saved in your browser. Use *Backup and restore* to copy them into a file and load them back.
- **Copy transcript** to paste a conversation into the real tools.

## Honest limits

- It is a **simulation**. The programs are animated actors following a script. No real AI or web search runs.
- The sample sources are real lines from the RedMed `AGENTS.md` notes. A question they do not cover gets the fixed sentence "The provided source documentation does not contain enough verified information to confidently answer …".
- Skills are stored in this browser only. Back them up.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts when you are online. Offline, the page falls back to your system fonts and still works.
- It cannot confirm that a source is the "latest". The sample lines have no dates, and the answer says so.
